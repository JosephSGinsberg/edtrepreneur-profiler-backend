Module 6 — Action & Exchange

The final module. Start ten real conversations, run your seven-day plan, write your Value Page.

Conversation opener

“Hi [Name]. I’m exploring a practical way to help [type of person] with [problem]. Could I ask you two or three short questions about how you currently handle this? No pressure to buy anything.”

Questions to ask

  • When did you last experience this problem?
  • What did you do about it?
  • What was frustrating or time-consuming?
  • What would a useful solution need to do?
  • May I contact you again if I develop something relevant?

Respect a no or no response. Keep personal information private and record only what you need.
